# Landing-date slips: how much changes if the landing moves 1–2 weeks? (card 19)

Generated by `node pipeline/date_slip.mjs` + `python pipeline/date_slip_doc.py`. Per-site data: `web/data/date_slip/<ID>.json` (contract section 15, proposal). Summary: `docs/date_slip_summary.json`.

**Method.** Every landing day 2026-01-01 … 2027-12-31 (00:00 UTC, 730 days), metrics over the first 14 days after landing, hourly, with the actual `web/js/core.js` definitions (sunlit = ≥ 50 % of the solar disc above the real terrain horizon; Earth = Earth centre above it; both = both at once; longest dark = longest run of not-sunlit hours). Best / worst = highest / lowest *both* (ties: shorter longest dark), the same ranking as the app's landing-date scan. Slip change = metric(landing + 7 or 14 days) − metric(landing) over every landing day; *median* = median absolute change, *worst* = the most unfavourable change.

## Headlines (generated from the numbers)

1. At 26 of 33 sites, a 14-day slip can turn the first two weeks on the surface from daylight into night: the longest darkness grows by up to 336 h.
2. At ARTEMIS_HAWORTH a 14-day slip changes the longest darkness by up to 297 h and a 7-day slip by up to 168 h; the share of time with both sunlight and an Earth link can drop by 92 percentage points.
3. SHACKLETON_RIM is the most slip-tolerant site: even in the worst case a 14-day slip adds only 59 h to the longest darkness.
4. For a typical landing day, sunlight in the first two weeks changes by a median of 48 percentage points after a 7-day slip and 52 after a 14-day slip (median over all 33 sites).
5. No landing date helps the Earth link at CHANGE4, CHANGE6: the Earth is never above the horizon there, so a direct link is impossible on every date.

## Limits
- Landing at 00:00 UTC of each day; a landing later in the day shifts every number slightly.
- Geometry only: terrain horizons from LOLA (cards 8/9), no link budget, no lander power model.
- Ephemeris window 2026–2030; landing days are limited to 2026–2027 so every 14-day window is inside it.
