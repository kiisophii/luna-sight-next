## 2b. Robotic landers: our first local sunset vs reported end of operations

**End of operations is an approximate proxy, not an exact measurement of sunset.** Operators often power down before nightfall (battery, thermal or schedule reasons), and some reports give only a date. Ours: first minute after landing when < 50 % of the solar disc is above the real terrain horizon (the app's `core.js` sunlit rule).

## Limits

- Apollo reference values are a published computation (ALSJ), not an in-situ measurement.
- Robotic end-of-operations times are proxies; the SLIM and Chang'e 3 dates come from secondary news reports (no agency page with the date/time was found). SLIM landed nose-down and IM-1 tipped over, so their power-down need not coincide with terrain sunset.
- Landing hours for IM-1 and Chang'e 3 are the widely reported touchdown times; the cited pages give the date. The sunset comparison depends on the landing day, not the hour.
- Non-Apollo landing-site datums are unverified (catalogue cards 4/15); horizons are from cards 8/9.
