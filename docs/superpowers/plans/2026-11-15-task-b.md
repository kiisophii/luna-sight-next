**Spec:** docs/morning_tasks_1115.md, task B; docs/data_contract.md section 18.

**Architecture:** A standalone verifier imports no repository production or verification modules. It decodes original SLDEM NPZ and PDS global rasters, uses Cartesian great-circle directions and full rays without early stopping, and evaluates the hourly ephemeris in its own NumPy loop. Optional CuPy array operations accelerate the full rays. Compare GPU results with a separate CPU PROJ/ECEF reference before the full run. Existing fetch_global.py is used only to acquire NASA bytes, never in the verifier.

**Constraints:** All 17 sites, 121 nodes/site, 720 azimuths, 4,001 geometric samples from 200 m to 300 km, curvature and 2 m observer. SLDEM to 150 km per observer, LDEM64/16 fallback; no SLDEM for CHANDRAYAAN3. Hourly 2026–2030. Fractions <=1 pp; darkness differences explicitly reported. Full checkpoints keyed by code/data/ephemeris/output identities. Reserve two physical CPU cores. No production changes. Source and AI usage logs required.
