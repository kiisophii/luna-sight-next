## How to read the terms

**REVIEW** means the repository does not establish a clear, item-specific licence or reuse grant. “Public”, “public archive” and an HTTP success are not licence determinations. **PENDING** preserves a specific unresolved permission question. Original software is [Apache-2.0](../LICENSE); this does not relicense source data, papers, fonts, software dependencies or hosted services. Upstream software licence names below describe their published terms, not a complete audit of every installed binary's bundled notices.

No NASA logos, seals or insignia are included in this document. Agency names are text attribution, not endorsement. Numeric facts were transcribed; paper figures are not reproduced here. Raw DEMs and kernels remain in ignored local caches. Third-party notices remain applicable to derived tiles and distributed code.
